# Questions bank (Phase 2)

Ask **only** what the brief cannot answer. Max 3–4 questions per block. Offer suggested options pre-filled from the brief; always allow a free answer. Ask in the user's language.
If the user skips a question: use the placeholder shown, add it to `config.pending_decisions`, and tell the user.

## Block A — Audience & value
1. Who is the ideal user? *(options from README/strings, e.g. "Beginners with 1–5 plants", "Collectors", "Busy parents")* — placeholder: `[[AUDIENCE]]`
2. What do they want to achieve, and what frustrates them today? *(e.g. "Stop killing plants" / "Forget to water")*
3. What is the main **outcome** (not features) and the **aha moment**? *(e.g. "First correct watering reminder", "Plant identified in 3 s")*

## Block B — Free vs premium
4. Confirm free vs premium split *(table from brief, marked TO CONFIRM)*.
5. Why is premium worth paying for, in one sentence?

## Block C — Pricing
6. Model: monthly / annual / lifetime / weekly — which ones? Highlighted plan? *(default: annual highlighted)*
7. Free trial? How many days, on which plans? Reminder before charge (does the app actually send one)?
8. Real prices and currency per plan, any intro discount. — placeholder: `$X.XX`
9. Is any offer time-limited **for real**? (If not, no urgency elements.)

## Block D — Objections & proof
10. Typical objections? *(price · distrust · "not now" · fear of surprise charges · "free is enough")*
11. Real social proof available? *(store rating + count, user count, testimonials with permission, press with rights)* — placeholder: `[[REAL RATING PENDING]]`

## Block E — Output format
12. Devices: iPhone, Android, both? *(default both: 1290×2796 and 1080×2400)*
13. Language(s), light/dark/both, phone frame yes/no *(default: app language, both themes, no frame)*
14. How many onboarding screens and paywalls; how many A/B variants per paywall? *(default: 5 onboarding, 2 paywalls, 2 variants each)*
15. Where does the paywall appear? *(end of onboarding · after a value moment · on locked feature tap · several)*

## Block F — Legal (only if missing)
16. Terms of use URL and Privacy policy URL. — placeholder: shown as link text, listed in pending.
